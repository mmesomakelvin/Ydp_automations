# 8. Unresponsive Mentor Nudge

**Status:** Design approved 2026-08-07 · Not yet implemented
**Lives in:** `matching/YDP Matching Automation.gs` (Google Apps Script) + the **Matched Pairs** sheet
**Depends on:** the live Mentorship Hub (`https://ydp-automations.vercel.app`) for full mentee details

---

## Problem

During the cohort, mentees message the facilitator (on WhatsApp) to say they
can't reach their mentor — e.g. *"I haven't gotten a response from my mentor.
Mentor: Linson Abah. Mentee ID: YDP-C2-Mentee-060. Name: Oluwanifemi Adeoti."*
The facilitator needs a fast, low-effort way to nudge that specific mentor, with
follow-up pressure that ramps up if the mentor stays silent — without manually
composing emails or tracking who has been chased how many times.

## Goal

One-flag, one-click nudging of unresponsive mentors, driven entirely off the
**Matched Pairs** sheet, with automatic escalation, an audit trail, and copies to
the facilitator and reassurance to the mentee.

---

## End-to-end flow

1. A mentee reports (via WhatsApp) that they can't reach their mentor.
2. The facilitator finds that mentee's row on **Matched Pairs** and types **`x`**
   in the new **Needs Nudge** column.
3. The facilitator clicks **YDP Matching → Send mentor nudges (flagged)**.
4. For each mentor with ≥1 flagged mentee: the mentor gets **one** email naming
   **only the flagged mentee(s)** (never the full roster), with that mentee's
   contact + Hub link. The facilitator is **CC'd**. Each flagged **mentee** gets a
   short "we've reminded your mentor" reassurance email.
5. The script records the send and **clears the `x`**, leaving the escalation
   memory in the tracking columns.

---

## Trigger: the `Needs Nudge` flag

- The facilitator flags a mentee by typing **`x`** (any non-empty value) in that
  mentee's **Needs Nudge** cell on Matched Pairs.
- An `x` means **"pending — nudge this pair now."** A blank cell sends nothing.
- After a successful send, the script **auto-clears the `x`**. The facilitator
  never types `xx` or manages dates — a second complaint later is just a single
  `x` again, and the stored **Nudge Count** makes that send one tier firmer.
- Rationale for auto-clear: a standing `x` would be ambiguous (already-sent vs.
  new request). Clearing removes that ambiguity; the tracking columns preserve
  the history.

## New columns on Matched Pairs (added to the right, non-destructive)

| Column | Written by | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **Needs Nudge** | Facilitator | Type `x` to flag; script clears it after sending. |
| **Nudge Count** | Script | Per-pair count; increments each send; drives escalation tone. |
| **Last Nudged At** | Script | Timestamp of the most recent nudge. |
| **Nudge Status** | Script | `SENT` / `ERROR`, for the audit trail. |

To **reset** a pair back to the warm tier (e.g. they reconnected, then drifted
again much later), the facilitator clears that pair's **Nudge Count** cell.

---

## The mentor nudge email

- **One email per mentor**, listing **only the flagged mentee(s)** for that
  mentor (aggregation is purely to avoid sending the mentor several emails at
  once — it never pulls in un-flagged mentees).
- For each named mentee: **email + phone + Hub link**. Phone is looked up
  best-effort from the **Mentee Source Snapshot** (by email); email and the Hub
  link are always present.
- Framing is **"[Mentee] is trying to reach you,"** never "[Mentee] complained."
- **CC:** the facilitator (the account running the send) on every mentor nudge.

### Escalation — driven by per-pair Nudge Count *over time*

Escalation is **repeat-over-time**, based on the highest **Nudge Count** among the
mentor's flagged mentees for this send. It is **not** driven by how many mentees a
mentor has, nor by how many complain at once.

| Count (after this send) | Tier | Tone |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Warm** | *"Your mentee [Name] is trying to reach you — here's how to say hello."* |
| 2 | **Firmer** | Direct check-in; notes the facilitator is aware (CC'd); asks them to reach out within a few days. |
| 3+ | **Escalation notice** | States YDP may reassign the mentee if contact isn't made; asks them to confirm availability. |

---

## The copies

- **Facilitator:** CC'd on every mentor nudge — an inbox record of who was
  contacted, when, and at what tier.
- **Reporting mentee:** a separate short reassurance email — *"Thanks for letting
  us know — we've reminded [Mentor] and asked them to reach out. If you still
  don't hear back in a few days, tell us again."* Sent to each flagged mentee.

---

## Menu buttons (mirrors the existing email tools)

Under **YDP Matching**:

- **Preview mentor nudge** — renders the email (first flagged pair, or a sample)
  in a dialog; no send, no tracking change.
- **Send mentor nudge — TEST to me** — sends a sample to the runner's own inbox
  only; no tracking change.
- **Send mentor nudges (flagged)** — the live send. For every mentor with a
  `Needs Nudge` flag: build the aggregated, escalated email; send to mentor (CC
  facilitator); send each flagged mentee their reassurance; then per flagged row
  increment **Nudge Count**, stamp **Last Nudged At**, set **Nudge Status = SENT**,
  and **clear the `x`**. On failure, set **Nudge Status = ERROR** and report it.

---

## Edge cases

- **Flagged mentee whose mentor has no valid email** → skip that mentee, record
  `ERROR`, and surface it in the run summary (never fail silently).
- **Gmail daily quota** checked before sending (as the other bulk sends do); if
  insufficient, send nothing and tell the facilitator.
- **Mentee phone not found** in the snapshot → omit phone; email + Hub link still
  carry the mentor to full details on the Hub.
- **Mentors with no mentees** (e.g. Ebenezer) can never be flagged — they have no
  Matched Pairs rows.
- **Nothing flagged** → the live send reports "no mentees are flagged" and does
  nothing.

---

## Out of scope (YAGNI)

- No automatic detection of unresponsiveness (no read receipts / activity
  tracking) — the facilitator flags based on mentee reports.
- No WhatsApp automation — the mentee reports via WhatsApp; the system nudges the
  mentor by email (the channel already built).
- No auto-reassignment — the 3rd tier only *warns* of possible reassignment;
  actual reassignment stays a manual facilitator decision.
